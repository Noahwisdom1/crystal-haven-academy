What We Offer

Our Academic Programmes

A continuous Nigerian-British curriculum journey from Crèche through Secondary School — structured for every stage of your child's development.

Our Approach

The Nigerian-British Curriculum

We combine the structure of the Nigerian national curriculum with the learner-centred breadth of the British educational framework — giving every child local grounding and global readiness.

Academic Rigour

Clear learning objectives, progressive milestones and regular assessment keep every learner on track.

Critical Thinking

Lessons designed to build reasoning, enquiry and independent problem-solving from the early years.

Creative Arts

Music, art and creative expression are woven into the weekly timetable at every level.

Character Formation

Values of integrity, respect and responsibility are developed alongside academic skills.

Ages 0 – 2

Crèche

Our Crèche provides a warm, safe and nurturing space for our youngest learners. Every interaction is designed to support sensory exploration, early bonding, motor development and the beginnings of social discovery — in an environment where children feel completely secure.

  • Safe, stimulating and age-appropriate learning environment
  • Focus on sensory play and motor skill development
  • Responsive, attentive care from dedicated educators
  • Regular updates and communication with parents

Ages 3 – 5

Early Years

Our Early Years programme uses structured, play-based learning to build the foundational skills every child needs. Language, early numeracy, creative thinking and social skills are developed through purposeful activities that keep young minds engaged and curious.

  • Structured play-based learning framework
  • Language and early literacy development
  • Introduction to numbers, shapes and patterns
  • Social skills, sharing and cooperative learning
  • Creative arts, music and physical activity
Enquire About Early Years
Ages 6 – 11

Primary School

Primary school at Crystal Haven Academy delivers a broad, balanced curriculum that covers all core subjects alongside the arts, technology and physical education. Our teachers create engaging lessons that challenge pupils at the right level, building confidence and a genuine love of learning.

  • English Language, Literature and Communication
  • Mathematics and numeracy skills
  • Basic Science and Technology
  • Social Studies and Cultural awareness
  • Creative Arts, Music and Physical Education
  • Digital literacy and ICT introduction
Enquire About Primary
Ages 12 – 17

Secondary School

Our Secondary School programme prepares students for national examinations while equipping them with the broader skills they need for higher education and life. Alongside rigorous academic study, students benefit from leadership development, co-curricular activities and career awareness programmes.

  • Preparation for national examinations
  • Sciences, Humanities and Social Sciences
  • English Language and Literature
  • Mathematics and Further Mathematics
  • Leadership and personal development
  • Co-curricular activities and career awareness
