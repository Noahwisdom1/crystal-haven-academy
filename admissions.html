The Process

How to Apply

Our admissions process is straightforward. Contact us to begin, and our team will guide you through each step.

01
Submit an Enquiry

Contact us by WhatsApp, email or in person to express your interest and request an admission form.

02
Campus Visit & Assessment

Schedule a visit to tour the school. A placement assessment will be arranged where applicable to identify the right class for your child.

03
Complete Your Application

Return the completed admission form together with the required documents listed below.

04
Offer & Enrolment

Receive your offer letter, complete the enrolment process and confirm your child's place for the new session.

Required Documents

The list below reflects standard Nigerian school admission requirements. Please contact us to confirm the exact documents required for your child's application.

  • Completed admission form (collected from the school)
  • Child's birth certificate or age declaration
  • Recent passport photographs of the child
  • Most recent school report or progress record (where applicable)
  • Parent or guardian's valid ID
  • Immunisation / health records (for Crèche and Early Years)
