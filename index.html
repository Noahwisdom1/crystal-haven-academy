About Us

A Place Where Children Thrive

Crystal Haven Academy is a co-educational Nigerian-British curriculum school established on 5 September 2023, located at Plot A1 & A2, Dr Sam Nujuma Estate, Galadimawa, Abuja. We are committed to providing quality education from the earliest years through secondary school, guided by our motto: Achieving Excellence Together.

We believe every child deserves an environment that is safe, stimulating, and supportive. Our curriculum blends academic rigour with character development, creative expression, and physical well-being — equipping each learner with the confidence and competence to succeed in a rapidly changing world.

  • Nigerian-British Curriculum

    A structured curriculum that combines Nigerian national standards with British educational frameworks.

  • Values-Centred Learning

    Integrity, respect, and responsibility are woven into daily school life at every level.

  • Inclusive Community

    A welcoming community where every child belongs and every family is a partner in learning.
