Our Story

Founded on a Vision of Excellence

Crystal Haven Academy was established on 5 September 2023 at Plot A1 & A2, Dr Sam Nujuma Estate, Galadimawa, Abuja. From its founding, the school has been guided by a clear and unwavering purpose: to deliver quality, holistic education that prepares each child for a successful and fulfilling life.

We operate a Nigerian-British curriculum — combining the structure and national standards of the Nigerian education system with the breadth, rigour and learner-centred philosophy of the British framework. This dual approach equips our students with both local grounding and global readiness.

Our motto, Achieving Excellence Together, reflects our belief that great outcomes are built through partnership — between teachers and students, between school and home, and among the learners themselves.

How We Teach

The Nigerian-British Curriculum

Our curriculum draws on the best of two strong educational traditions. The Nigerian national framework ensures our students meet local academic standards and are well-prepared for national assessments. The British framework adds a broader perspective — emphasising critical thinking, creative expression, and independent learning skills.

Together, these approaches produce learners who are both locally grounded and internationally competitive.

  • Structured Learning

    Clear learning objectives and progressive milestones at every stage.

  • Critical Thinking

    Lessons designed to develop reasoning, problem-solving and independent thought.

  • Creative Expression

    Arts, music and creative activities woven into the daily learning experience.
