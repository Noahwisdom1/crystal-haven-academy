School Facilities

Spaces designed to support every dimension of student life — academic, creative, physical and social.

The Learning Environment

A Campus Built for Growth

Every space at Crystal Haven Academy has been thoughtfully arranged to create an environment where children feel safe, stimulated and ready to learn. The details below reflect what is currently in place at our Galadimawa campus. We will update this page as our facilities continue to develop.

Classrooms

Purpose-designed learning spaces, furnished to create a productive and comfortable atmosphere for all age groups, from Early Years through Secondary.

Library & Reading Room

A reading space stocked with age-appropriate books and resources to encourage independent learning and a love of reading.

ICT Facilities

Computer resources supporting digital literacy and technology education from the early years upwards — preparing students for a technology-driven world.

Play & Sports Area

Open outdoor space for physical activity, supervised play and sport — supporting health, teamwork and physical well-being across all age groups.

School Transport

Convenient school transport services supporting students' journeys to and from Crystal Haven Academy.

Administrative Block

A dedicated reception and administrative area where parents and visitors are welcomed, enquiries are handled, and school operations are managed.
